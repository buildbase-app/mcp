# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report security problems in public issues, pull requests or
discussions.

Email support@buildbase.app with "SECURITY" in the subject line. Tell us:

- what you found and where,
- the steps to reproduce it,
- what someone could do with it.

Do not include access tokens, passwords or other people's data in your report.

We aim to acknowledge your report within 3 business days. We will keep you
updated while we work on a fix.

## Scope

- The hosted MCP server at `https://api.console.buildbase.app/mcp` and its
  OAuth sign-in flow.
- The files in this repository.
